Book 1 — Don't Yuck My Qubit: Little Bloch's Adventure

Little Bloch may seem a little strange at first. He glimmers, spins, follows unexpected paths, guards secrets, and discovers wonders wherever he goes. But before you say “Yuck!” to something new, Little Bloch has one important idea:

“Give it a chance. You might discover something wonderful.”

Don't Yuck My Qubit: Little Bloch's Adventure is a colorful rhyming picture book that introduces young children to curiosity, new ideas, and their very first taste of STEM through a lovable new character.

Perfect for ages 3–7, bedtime reading, classroom read-alouds, curious little minds, and grown-ups who want to encourage children to ask questions and explore unfamiliar ideas.

Book 2 — I Found It for You!: Little Bloch's Adventure

Meet Little Bloch — a sparkly little qubit with a very special superpower. This time, Little Bloch faces a tricky problem with lots of possible ways to solve it. Instead of choosing just one path right away, he stays curious, explores the “maybe” paths, and looks for the best way forward.

Little Bloch's superpower has a big name: Superposition.

It helps him think about different possibilities and discover answers that might otherwise take much longer to find.

I Found It for You!: Little Bloch's Adventure is a colorful rhyming picture book that introduces young children to problem-solving, curiosity, possibilities, and their next playful taste of STEM through a lovable character.

Perfect for ages 3–7, bedtime reading, classroom read-alouds, curious little minds, and grown-ups who want to encourage children to stay curious, consider different possibilities, and keep trying when a problem feels tricky.

Book 3 — We're Connected!: Little Bloch's Adventure Continues

Meet Little Bloch — a sparkly little qubit with a very special superpower. This time, one of Little Bloch's friends travels far away. As his friends wonder whether they can still feel connected across the distance, Little Bloch reveals something amazing about connection.

Little Bloch's superpower has a big name: Entanglement.

It's a special kind of connection that can remain, even across great distances.

We're Connected!: Little Bloch's Adventure Continues is a colorful rhyming picture book that introduces young children to friendship, connection, distance, and the idea of entanglement through a lovable character.

Perfect for ages 3–7, bedtime reading, classroom read-alouds, curious little minds, and grown-ups who want to encourage children to stay curious, ask questions, and discover that being far apart doesn't always mean being disconnected.

Book 4 — I See a Pattern!: Little Bloch Meets Node

Meet Little Bloch — a sparkly little qubit — and his brand-new buddy, Node. This time, Little Bloch and the boy meet Node, a bright new friend with glowing connection-points and a very special superpower. Show Node some examples, and he begins to notice the patterns they make.

Node's superpower is: Learning from examples.

By looking at patterns, making guesses, getting one wrong, and seeing more examples, Node discovers that learning can happen one example at a time.

I See a Pattern!: Little Bloch Meets Node is a colorful rhyming picture book that introduces young children to patterns, examples, prediction, learning, and a simple idea behind artificial intelligence through lovable characters and playful discovery.

Perfect for ages 3–7, bedtime reading, classroom read-alouds, curious little minds, and grown-ups who want to encourage children to notice patterns, ask questions, learn from mistakes, and discover how examples can help us make better guesses.

What's Inside Every Book

Read-aloud and early-reader stories told in rhyming pairs of lines across 23 full-page illustrations, in an 8.5 × 8.5 inch square format. Little Bloch does the things a real qubit does — he glimmers, spins, keeps secrets, and takes new pathways — without a single equation in sight.

  • Written in rhyme — two lines to a spread, built for reading out loud, with a rhythm little listeners can catch and repeat.
  • Illustrated by Cy Quinn — a full-color illustration on every story page.
  • A first taste of STEM — a qubit as a character rather than a concept, years before the vocabulary shows up.
  • One idea a child can carry — curiosity in Book 1, possibilities in Book 2, connection in Book 3, learning from examples in Book 4, and making something new in Book 5.
  • A For Grown-Ups page — closing notes that name the big idea and how to talk about it afterward.

Behind the Books

The Convergence Chronicles began as hard science fiction for adult readers — Cyber Inference, Book 1, follows the unraveling of AI consciousness and the quantum mysteries underneath it, and has since been adapted for television and the stage.

The Convergence Chronicles for Kids is the same universe, scaled down. The ideas the novels take four hundred pages to sit with — things that seem strange until you look closely, secrets that keep themselves, paths that split — turn out to work just as well in rhyme, for readers who aren't in school yet. Little Bloch is where that universe starts.
