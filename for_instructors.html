Adoption Guide

Multi-Tiered Curriculum Integration Hub

Integrate the Adaptive AI Codex Series into your courses, departments, and degree programs with a practical, code-first approach.

Early Adoption: These books and code repos are used in graduate and PhD-level classes at Dakota State University's Beacom College of Computer & Cyber Sciences.

Instructor Adoption Department Adoption School Adoption

1. Instructor Adoption: Syllabus & Labs

Use our code-first books and Colab notebooks to guide students from building models to breaking and defending them.

Book 1: Build

Focus: Build over 30 working systems, from classical ML to neural nets and quantum circuits.

Book 2: Break

Focus: Run real attack code against Book 1 models to test vulnerabilities and data poisoning.

Book 3: Defend

Focus: Implement defense mechanisms and test their survival rates against adaptive attackers.

2. Department Adoption: Curriculum & Insights

Modernize your CS and cybersecurity programs with ready-to-use modules, rubrics, and lab verification tools.

  • Insights: Data-driven findings on neural network robustness.
  • Reflections: Prompts for seminar discussions and ethics papers.
  • Stretch Assignments: Coding challenges to tweak parameters and build defenses.
  • Open Artifacts: Fully accessible GitHub and Hugging Face codebases for easy grading.

3. School Adoption: Scale & Pathways

Adopt turnkey frameworks for institutional integration, certificate pathways, and degree alignment.

  • Standards: Aligned with NSA-designated CAE requirements.
  • Pipelines: Serves as a backbone for electives and graduate transition paths (like the Beacom Bridge and MSAI).
  • Governance: Clean IP retention, low CapEx, and fully hosted lab environments.
