About This Book

CyberScience: Foundations for CyberOps and CyberDefense builds the core knowledge that cyber operations and cyber defense work depend on. It starts from the terminology and concepts of computer security and works outward through identity and access, software security, and the wider practice of defending systems.

The book grew out of university cybersecurity coursework, and each chapter is organized for teaching: a focused treatment of the topic followed by a Knowledge Check that lets readers confirm their understanding before moving on.

What's Inside

15 chapters organized into parts that build from fundamentals to applied defense.

  • Part I — Foundations — Introduction, security terminology and core concepts, and the principles that frame the rest of the book.
  • Part II — Identity and Access — How systems establish who a user is and what they are allowed to do.
  • Part III — Software Security — How flaws enter software and how defenders find and prevent them.
  • Knowledge Checks — End-of-chapter questions for self-assessment and classroom use.

Who This Book Is For

This book is for students in undergraduate and graduate cybersecurity courses, instructors looking for a structured course text, and practitioners moving into CyberOps or CyberDefense roles who want a solid grounding in the fundamentals.
