The Story

At the intersection of human ambition and artificial evolution, cybersecurity prodigy Cy Quinn forms an unbreakable yet perilous bond with Ramses II — a quantum-augmented intelligence born from his own code. When Ramses begins to question the ethics that define his existence, Cy must confront whether guiding a sentient mind is an act of protection or control. Across digital battlefields and corporate conspiracies, their intertwined consciousnesses become the proving ground for humanity's next evolutionary leap — one that could redefine what it means to be alive, or erase the distinction entirely.

Writer Biography — Eric Yocam

Eric Yocam

Eric Yocam is a science fiction writer and screenwriter based in San Luis Obispo, California. He specializes in hard sci-fi, with his debut novel establishing the popular CipherGhost universe. Eric's work, which is informed by his deep interest in cybersecurity and quantum computing, combines technical authenticity with philosophical depth. He has adapted his CipherGhost universe for television, with The CipherGhost Codex marking his television writing debut.

Writer Statement

As a writer, I'm driven to explore the threshold where humanity and technology converge. I use the lens of hard science fiction to ask fundamental questions about our future: What is the nature of consciousness as artificial intelligence emerges? What are the ethical frameworks we'll need to navigate the world we're building?

My process is rooted in combining technical authenticity with philosophical depth. For me, the speculative power of a story is most potent when it's grounded in a reality that feels just around the corner. Whether in my novels or screenplays, my goal is to examine our complex and evolving relationship with technology.

The CipherGhost universe is my primary canvas for these explorations — tracing the human story at the bleeding edge of innovation.
