Visited

Home
San Luis Obispo, CA 
Home base on California's Central Coast, between San Francisco and Los Angeles.
Europe
Paris, France 
France's capital and cultural center, known for the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, and centuries of art and architecture along the Seine.
London, England 
The United Kingdom's capital, home to the Tower of London, Westminster, and centuries of royal and political history along the Thames.
Munich, Germany 
The Bavarian capital, known for its historic Marienplatz, Oktoberfest tradition, and proximity to the Alps.
Africa
Cairo, Egypt 
Egypt's capital on the Nile, gateway to the Pyramids of Giza and thousands of years of ancient history.
Aswan, Egypt 
A southern Nile city known for the Aswan High Dam, Lake Nasser, and nearby ancient Nubian and Egyptian temples.
Asia
Hong Kong 
A dense, vertical harbor city blending British colonial history with a fast-moving modern skyline.
Macau 
A former Portuguese colony known for its blend of Chinese and European architecture and its casino resorts.
Shanghai, China 
China's largest city and financial hub, known for the futuristic Pudong skyline across the river from the historic Bund.
Jakarta, Indonesia 
Indonesia's sprawling capital, a hub of commerce and culture on the island of Java.
Bali, Indonesia 
Known for its surf breaks, terraced rice paddies, and Hindu temples set among volcanic landscapes.
New Delhi, India 
India's capital, where Mughal-era monuments sit alongside the seat of the modern Indian government.
Singapore 
A compact island city-state known for its efficiency, greenery, and mix of Chinese, Malay, and Indian cultures.
Bangkok, Thailand 
Thailand's capital, known for ornate temples, floating markets, and a famously vibrant street life.
Mexico
Cancún 
A Caribbean coast resort city on the Yucatán Peninsula known for its beaches and nearby Mayan ruins.
Puerto Vallarta 
A Pacific coast beach town set against the Sierra Madre mountains, known for its historic seaside boardwalk.
Puerto Peñasco (Rocky Point) 
A Sea of Cortéz fishing town popular with Southwest U.S. visitors for its beaches and sportfishing.
Tijuana 
A busy border city just south of San Diego, known for its food scene and cross-border culture.
Cabo San Lucas 
A resort town at the southern tip of the Baja California Peninsula, known for its rock arch and marina.
Caribbean / Atlantic
Bermuda 
A British Overseas Territory in the North Atlantic, known for its pink-sand beaches and pastel-colored architecture.
Nassau, Bahamas 
The Bahamian capital on New Providence Island, a longtime hub for Caribbean cruising and beach tourism.
Canada
Victoria, BC 
British Columbia's capital on Vancouver Island, known for its English-style gardens and harbor waterfront.
Vancouver, BC 
A Pacific Northwest coastal city framed by mountains, known for its mix of urban life and nearby wilderness.
