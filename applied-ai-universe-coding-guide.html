About This Book

The Applied AI Universe Coding Guide is a project-first, concept-second guide to the entire AI Universe. It is organized around the depiction of the AI Universe — five concentric rings, each labeling a major sub-field of AI.

Every chapter follows the same four-layer structure: Concept, Code Snippet, Output, and Insight. No prior AI experience is required — only intermediate Python skills.

What's Inside

  • Part II — Artificial Intelligence — Planning, Expert Systems, Fuzzy Logic
  • Part III — Machine Learning — Feature Engineering, Supervised/Unsupervised Learning
  • Part IV — Neural Networks — Perceptrons, Backpropagation, CNNs, LSTMs
  • Part V — Deep Learning — GANs, Attention, Transfer Learning, RL
